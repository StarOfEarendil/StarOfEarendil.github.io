Welcome

From Blueprints to Bandwidth

Hello! I’m a 32-year-old mature student currently navigating the Computer Systems Technician bridge program at Durham College, with my sights set on an Honours Bachelor of IT in Network and Security at Ontario Tech. My path to web development wasn't a straight line. I spent years as a dual-licensed machinist and millwright, mastering the physical side of how things work. Now, I’ve traded wrenches for keyboards, applying that same precision and troubleshooting logic to code. When I’m not buried in Java assignments or exploring network security, you can find me fueling my obsession with The Lord of the Rings (Extended Editions only, of course), catching the latest Formula 1 race, or hitting the trails with my dogs. I’m also a dedicated gamer and gym-goer, constantly leveling up both my character stats and my deadlift.

So don't waste time! Chase your dreams! :)
