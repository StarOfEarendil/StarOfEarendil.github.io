From Blueprints to Bandwidth

The "Version 1.0" Build: The Trades

My path to the world of technology was not a straight line; it was engineered. Before I ever wrote a line of Java or configured a VLAN, I spent over a decade working with my hands. I am a Dual-Licensed Machinist and Millwright, which means I spent my 20s diagnosing complex mechanical failures, reading technical blueprints, and working to tolerances measured in microns.

That career taught me an invaluable lesson: Systems are systems. Whether it’s a hydraulic gearbox or a Python script, the logic of troubleshooting remains the same. You isolate the variable, test the input, and verify the output. I am now applying that same industrial precision to the world of software and network security.

The "Version 2.0" Upgrade: Education & Hardware

I am currently a 32-year-old mature student in the Computer Systems Technician bridge program at Durham College, transferring to Ontario Tech University for my Honours Bachelor of IT in Networking and IT Security. But my education doesn't stop at the classroom door.

I am a hands-on builder. My pride and joy is my custom "Sleeper" Optiplex build. To the untrained eye, it looks like a boring office desktop from 2010. Inside, however, I’ve completely re-engineered it with modern high-performance components. Building it required not just PC knowledge, but the mechanical skill to modify the case for airflow and fitment. It’s the perfect physical representation of who I am: unassuming on the outside, but powerful and highly technical on the inside.

The Digital Playground: Gaming

When I’m not coding, I am usually immersed in open worlds. I have sunk thousands of hours into the lore of The Elder Scrolls and the wastelands of Fallout. I appreciate games that let you build your own story and explore massive, detailed environments (which probably explains why I love Grand Theft Auto so much).

For me, gaming isn't just entertainment; it's about systems. I love analyzing how game engines handle physics or how NPCs interact, constantly looking at the "matrix" behind the graphics.

Need for Speed: The Racing Obsession

If it has an engine and goes fast, I’m watching it. I am a massive motorsport fan with a split obsession between Formula 1 and IndyCar.

My loyalty lies with McLaren (Papaya Orange all the way!), and I am a huge supporter of Lando Norris. There is nothing quite like the engineering warfare of an F1 race weekend—watching teams analyze telemetry data in real-time is basically the ultimate IT project. I try to bring that same "pit wall" focus to my own studies.

Offline Mode

Balance is key. When I step away from the screen, I head for the trees. I am an avid hiker and love hitting the trails with my dog, my German Shepherd/Collie mix, Ace.

I also admit to being a Tolkien purist. I’ve read The Silmarillion more times than I can count, and I firmly believe the Extended Editions of The Lord of the Rings are the only way to watch.
